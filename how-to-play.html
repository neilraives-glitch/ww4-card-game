HOW TO PLAY

WORLD WAR 4

War, with six nations that cheat. Highest card takes the pile — unless the nation on it says otherwise.

A round

Three beats, every time
1
Everyone flips
One card off the top of each deck, face up, at the same time.
2
Totals are compared
Each side adds up its cards. 2 is low, Ace is high — then abilities are applied, and they can change the answer.
3
The winner takes everything
Every card on the table goes to the winning side's pile and counts toward their score.

The six nations

Each one bends a different rule
USA
First Strike
Wins a same-rank clash outright — but only if the other side isn't holding USA too.
ISRAEL
Iron Dome
Switches off the enemy's ability for the round. If both sides field Israel, the domes cancel and nobody's ability fires.
TAIWAN
Chokepoint
A same-rank clash re-flips instead of escalating. No antes, no cards at risk.
RUSSIA
Fortify
Lays 2 antes into a World War 4 instead of 4 — half the exposure when a tie goes nuclear.
CHINA
Mass Mobilization
Winning the round also seizes 2 extra cards straight out of the enemy's stock.
IRAN
Asymmetric
A 6 or lower beats a Jack, Queen, King or Ace outright. The small card wins.

Order of operations

When abilities collide, this is the order
FIRSTIron Dome
Resolves before anything else, which is what lets it suppress any ability below it.
THENChokepoint
De-escalates a clash before it can become a war.
THENFirst Strike
Settles the clash — if Chokepoint didn't already defuse it.
THENAsymmetric
Decided before the totals are compared, so it changes who wins.
THENFortify
Applies once a tie has already sent the round to war.
LASTMass Mobilization
Applies after the win is settled, taking its cut of the spoils.

Two kinds of war

What happens when nobody wins cleanly
⚡ World War 4
Both sides total the same — or both flip a Joker. Everyone lays 4 cards face down (2 with Fortify) and flips again. The pot carries over, so the next winner takes all of it.
⚔ Same-rank clash
Two opposing cards share a rank. Those players lay 2 cards and flip once — that single flip decides the whole pot. Chokepoint or First Strike can end it before it starts.

The Joker is the bomb. There are two in every deck. A lone Joker beats anything on the table. Two Jokers at once start a World War 4.

Win a round in team play with every flipped card from the same bloc and the score is multiplied. A Joker breaks alignment — it belongs to nobody. Team modes only.

Winning the war

Run out of cards and you're out. When every player on a side has an empty deck and an empty won pile, the other side takes the war.

Out of cards mid-game but still holding a won pile? It gets shuffled and becomes your new deck. You're only finished when both are empty.
